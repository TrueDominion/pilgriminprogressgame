# Rights and Licences

Not legal advice. Have a lawyer confirm anything that matters commercially.

## Source texts

| Work | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| John Bunyan, *The Pilgrim's Progress* (1678) | Public domain worldwide | Our modernised dialogue is our own adaptation and is protectable as such. |
| King James Version (1611) | Public domain in the US and most countries | **United Kingdom: Crown copyright via letters patent**, administered by Cambridge University Press. Short quotations in a game are customary, but commercial reproduction in the UK technically needs permission. Keep quotations short; consider writing to CUP before UK launch if the codex grows. |

## AI-generated assets

- Higgsfield's terms govern commercial use of its outputs; **verify** the current terms of your plan allow commercial use in a paid app and keep a copy of the terms with the date you accepted them.
- In the US, purely AI-generated material is generally not protected by copyright (US Copyright Office guidance, 2023 to 2025). Human selection, arrangement and modification can be. `design/assets.csv` records `human_modified` for every asset so we can show what a human changed.
- The game's code, level design, writing, adaptation, and the selection and arrangement of assets are human-authored.
