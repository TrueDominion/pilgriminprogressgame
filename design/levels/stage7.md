# Stage 7: Vanity Fair

**Source:** Part I, Vanity Fair, the merchants ("What will ye buy?"), "We buy the truth", the trial of Faithful, Hopeful joins.
**Target playtime:** 4 to 5 minutes. **Movement set:** free + armour. **Reward:** Hopeful.

## Mechanic: the stalls

- Stalls line the street. Merchants call out; a stall's pull field drags Christian toward it at 40 px/s while he is inside it. Walking or jumping through is easy if the player keeps moving.
- Standing at a stall opens "What will ye buy?" Each item costs Light (10 to 30). Buying adds weight: speed and jump drop by 8% per item, up to 3 items, and the sprite gains a bundle again (an echo of the burden, deliberately). The weight drops off at the next checkpoint, with a short line from Faithful.
- Answering "We buy the truth" to a merchant ends that stall's pull for the rest of the stage.

## Beat map

| # | Section | Tension | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entering the town | Low | Colourful, busy, loud music. Faithful walks with Christian. Checkpoint A. |
| 2 | The market street | Rising | Stalls with pull fields; rooftops offer a harder, stall-free route. |
| 3 | "We buy the truth" | Story | The crowd surrounds the pilgrims. Dialogue choice. The town riots; guards lead Christian and Faithful away. Checkpoint B. |
| 4 | The cage and the trial | Story | Handled off-screen and suitable for children: a short still with the townspeople, a judge's bench in silhouette, and narration that Faithful stood firm and "was taken up" to the Celestial Gate by a chariot (the book's own image). No depiction of his death. |
| 5 | Escape the town | Rising | Christian slips out over rooftops at night. Checkpoint C. |
| 6 | Hopeful | Release | Hopeful catches up outside the walls: he saw Faithful's courage and chose to follow. Companion tutorial: he follows, and his hint button explains. Stage card. |

## Hazards and answers

| Hazard | Answer |
|---|---|
| Stall pull fields | Keep moving, or use the rooftops |
| Bought weight | Refuse; it falls off at the next checkpoint |
| Guards at night | Timing and rooftops (no combat with people) |

## Scrolls

1. On the rooftop route. Codex: Proverbs 23:23.
2. Inside the one stall that sells nothing (an empty booth), reached only by declining every merchant before it. Codex: Bunyan, "What will ye buy?" / "We buy the truth."
3. Left by Faithful in the cage. Codex: Revelation 2:10.

## Notes and sensitivities

- The fair is about vanity, not any real culture or faith. Merchants and goods are generic and fantastical (crowns, titles, sweets, mirrors). No real religious symbols, no caricatured ethnicities.
- Faithful's trial: no execution shown or described in detail. Narration and one still only.
