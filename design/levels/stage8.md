# Stage 8: Doubting Castle

**Source:** Part I, By-Path Meadow, Vain-confidence, Giant Despair and Diffidence, the dungeon, the Key called Promise.
**Target playtime:** 5 to 6 minutes. **Movement set:** free + armour + Hopeful. **Reward:** Key called Promise.

## Beat map

| # | Section | Tension | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The rough road | Low | The road is stony; By-Path Meadow over a stile looks soft and easy. Christian chooses it (scripted, Hopeful objects). Checkpoint A. |
| 2 | By-Path Meadow | Rising | Pleasant at first, then rain and night. A man named Vain-confidence runs ahead and falls into a pit (off-screen sound only). Flood water rises; the path back is cut. |
| 3 | Capture | Story | Exhausted, the pilgrims sleep in a field; Giant Despair finds them. Cut to the dungeon. Checkpoint B (cell). |
| 4 | The dungeon | Low dread | The Giant's taunts at the door (no depiction of beatings; he "rages" and slams the door). Hopeful encourages. Christian remembers the Key called Promise in his bosom. The Key opens the cell. |
| 5 | Escape: stealth | High | Corridors, the castle yard, three locked gates. Giant Despair patrols with a lantern and a visible sight cone. Being seen sends both pilgrims back to the last door checkpoint (C, D). High windows cast sunlight shafts; luring him into one stuns him for 3 s. Hopeful whispers hints when the player is stuck for 20 s. |
| 6 | The iron gate | Climax | The last lock grates loudly; the Giant wakes and charges, but sunlight floods the yard as the gate opens and he collapses, stunned. The pilgrims run. Stage card with the pillar they set up to warn others. |

## Hazards and answers

| Hazard | Answer |
|---|---|
| Rising flood | Keep moving forward; no way back |
| Locked doors | Key called Promise |
| Giant Despair's patrols | Stealth, timing, sunlight stun |

## Checkpoints

A: road. B: cell. C, D: each of the first two gates. Being caught never costs Hope.

## Scrolls

1. On the stile before By-Path Meadow (a last warning). Codex: Jeremiah 31:21.
2. In the dungeon, behind loose straw. Codex: 2 Peter 1:4.
3. In the castle yard, in a sunlit corner that requires a stun to reach. Codex: Bunyan, "What a fool am I..."

## Notes

- Giant Despair: big, slow, grumpy, a little comic. Not horrifying. His wife Diffidence is mentioned in dialogue only.
- The suicide theme in Bunyan's text (the Giant urges them to end their lives) is omitted entirely. **[content decision for the 8+ audience; owner to confirm]**
