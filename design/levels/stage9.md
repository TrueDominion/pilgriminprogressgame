# Stage 9: To the Celestial City

**Source:** Part I, the Delectable Mountains and the shepherds' perspective glass, the Enchanted Ground, the land of Beulah, the River, the Shining Ones, the gate of the City.
**Target playtime:** 5 to 6 minutes. **Movement set:** free + armour + Hopeful. **Reward:** finale and credits.

## Beat map

| # | Section | Tension | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delectable Mountains | Breather | Bright, gentle platforming across meadows and orchards. The four shepherds (Knowledge, Experience, Watchful, Sincere) in one combined dialogue. Checkpoint A. |
| 2 | The perspective glass | Wonder | At the Hill Clear, the player uses the telescope (Action). The camera pans far right across parallax layers to a shimmering glimpse of the City, then back. Hands "shake" (gentle wobble, off if screen shake is off). |
| 3 | The Enchanted Ground | Rising | Drowsy air. If Christian stops moving for 2 s, the screen dims; at 5 s he falls asleep and the player returns to the last checkpoint. Talking with Hopeful (Action near him) resets the dimming. The conversation lines are short Bunyan excerpts. Checkpoint B and C. |
| 4 | Beulah | Breather | Light, flowers, birdsong, the City close on the horizon. No hazards. |
| 5 | The River | Climax | No bridge. Wading. The water rises while the player hesitates and falls while he moves on. Christian struggles; at the deepest point Hopeful holds him up: "Be of good cheer, my brother, I feel the bottom, and it is good." The player cannot fail here once Hopeful catches him. Checkpoint D before. |
| 6 | The gate | Finale | The Shining Ones. Christian and Hopeful hand in their certificates (the Roll). Trumpets. Finale cutscene: the gate opens to light. Credits roll over a scrolling illustration of the whole road. |

## Hazards and answers

| Hazard | Answer |
|---|---|
| Enchanted Ground | Keep moving; talk with Hopeful |
| The River | Keep moving; Hopeful's help |

## Scrolls

1. Delectable Mountains, on Mount Caution's edge. Codex: Bunyan on the shepherds.
2. In the Enchanted Ground, reached only while talking with Hopeful (the dimming must be held off). Codex: 1 Thessalonians 5:6.
3. In Beulah. Codex: Isaiah 62:4.

## After the credits

The codex shows the total Light and Scrolls. All 27 Scrolls unlocks the epilogue illustration. The title screen changes to the City at dawn.

## Notes

- The River is about death in the book. Keep it as a crossing, not a drowning. Hopeful's line carries it.
- The finale is the second emotional peak. It shares the Cross's music motif.
