# Stage 5: Hill Difficulty and the Palace Beautiful

**Source:** Part I, Formalist and Hypocrisy, the paths Danger and Destruction, the arbour and the lost Roll, Timorous and Mistrust, the chained lions, Watchful the porter, the Palace Beautiful and its armoury.
**Target playtime:** 5 to 6 minutes. **Movement set:** free. **Reward:** Sword, Shield, Helmet.

## Beat map

| # | Section | Tension | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foot of the hill | Low | Formalist and Hypocrisy tumble over the wall (they skipped the gate). Three paths: Difficulty (straight up), Danger (left), Destruction (right). Checkpoint A. |
| 2 | Side paths (traps) | Lesson | Danger: an easy wide road into a dark wood that loops the player back to the junction. Destruction: an easy road ending in a slide down to the base. Each costs time, not Hope, and Formalist / Hypocrisy are seen lost on them. |
| 3 | The climb | Rising | Vertical section, 6 screens tall. Double jumps, wall ledges, wind gusts near the top. Checkpoint B mid-climb. |
| 4 | The arbour | Choice | A rest point halfway. Sitting restores full Hope. Staying more than 4 seconds makes Christian doze; on waking, the Roll is missing and the player must later backtrack to fetch it. Clear warning: the screen warms and slows as the doze approaches. Checkpoint C. |
| 5 | The lost Roll (if dozed) | Backtrack | Near the top, Christian realises the Roll is gone. A short descent back to the arbour, then the climb again by a quicker side route. About 40 seconds of cost. |
| 6 | Timorous and Mistrust | Story | Two men running down: "There are lions!" Dialogue. |
| 7 | The lions | High | A narrow path with a chained lion on each side. Their reach is shown by the chain. The middle 2 tiles are always safe. Roaring is loud but harmless; touching a paw costs 1 Hope. Watchful the porter calls: "Keep in the midst of the path." Checkpoint D before. |
| 8 | The armoury | Reward | Palace Beautiful. Short scene with Discretion, Prudence, Piety and Charity (one combined dialogue). The armoury: Sword (attack tutorial on training dummies), Shield (block tutorial against thrown fruit), Helmet (Hope +1). Stage card. |

## Hazards and answers

| Hazard | Answer |
|---|---|
| Steep vertical climb | Double jump |
| Easy side paths | Read the signposts; choose Difficulty |
| The arbour | Rest briefly, leave |
| Chained lions | Keep to the middle |

## Checkpoints

A: junction. B: mid-climb. C: arbour. D: before the lions.

## Scrolls

1. At the end of the Danger path (the only reward there, and a lesson): Codex: Proverbs 14:12.
2. On a wind-assisted ledge near the top of the climb. Codex: Bunyan, "The hill, though high, I covet to ascend."
3. In the Palace library after the armoury. Codex: Ephesians 6:13.

## Notes

- The arbour must be fair: the doze warning starts at 2 s, and players who stand up before 4 s keep the Roll.
- The armoury is also the combat tutorial for stage 6. Keep it short and clear.
